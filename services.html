Our Services

At Phantom Impact Consulting (PIC), we offer a comprehensive suite of services designed to strengthen organizations
and drive sustainable impact. Through innovative, evidence-based approaches, we help our partners navigate
complex challenges with confidence and clarity.

Market and Social Research

We provide high-quality research to support strategic decision-making. Our work focuses on market trends, consumer behavior, social dynamics, and policy impacts.

Policy Development, Review, and Analysis

We support governments, NGOs, and international organizations in creating policies that are evidence-based, contextually relevant, and aligned with global best practices.

Organizational Capacity Assessment and Development

We help organizations grow by identifying strengths, addressing gaps, and improving performance. Our services include training programs, governance support, and institutional strengthening.

Program Design, Quality Assurance, and Evaluation

We ensure programs are impactful and effective from start to finish. Our expertise includes logical framework design, monitoring systems, and independent reviews.

Gender Equality and Social Inclusion (GESI)

We apply GESI principles to create inclusive programs and policies. Our work includes gender analyses, inclusive strategy development, and ensuring marginalized groups have equitable access to services.

Systems Review and Risk Management

We protect organizations through comprehensive systems analysis and proactive risk management, including vulnerability assessments, internal controls, and risk mitigation strategies.
