Our Experiences

Our consultants have successfully conducted assignments across Africa, the Middle East, and Asia, bringing expertise in diverse socio-economic and cultural contexts. With a proven track record of delivering high-quality research and advisory services, PIC is dedicated to fostering positive, lasting change. We partner with key stakeholders to bridge gaps, influence policy, and enhance the effectiveness of development programs, ensuring our solutions are both data-driven and human-centered.

Our Expertise

Disaster Risk Reduction & Resilience

Strengthening communities through climate adaptation, early warning systems, and youth-led disaster preparedness initiatives.

Gender, Protection & SRHR

Advancing equality through GBV prevention, economic empowerment, child protection, and youth-friendly SRHR services like family planning and menstrual health education.
