About Us

At Phantom Impact Consulting (PIC), we provide tailored, evidence-based solutions to help organizations navigate complex environments. Our expertise includes research, policy development, capacity assessment, program design, evaluation, GESI, systems review, and risk management, all aimed at driving sustainable impact.

We work with for-profit and nonprofit organizations across humanitarian, development, and peacebuilding sectors in Africa, the Middle East, Asia, and beyond. With over 50 years of combined experience, our multidisciplinary team partners with governments, international agencies, NGOs, and the private sector to deliver innovative, locally grounded solutions that create lasting, positive change.

Services

At Phantom Impact Consulting (PIC), we offer a diverse range of services designed to support organizations in achieving sustainable, high-impact outcomes. Through tailored research, strategic advisory, and capacity development, we help our partners address complex challenges, strengthen systems, and drive meaningful change across communities.
